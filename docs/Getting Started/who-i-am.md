Hi, I'm Jake. I've spent the last 10+ years being the person customers get handed to when the first answer didn't work.

Most of that time has been split between two things: talking to people who are frustrated and need a straight answer, and going deep enough into logs, APIs, and infrastructure to actually find one. I've debugged developer platform APIs at SmartThings, and right now at Bright AI I troubleshoot a 180+ device edge fleet end-to-end; APIs, cloud, Linux, root cause, all the way through.

Outside of work: I run a small home lab that's probably more elaborate than it needs to be — a self-hosted Fedora server, a pile of Podman containers, and a separate Home Assistant setup wired into an LLM API just because I wanted to see if I could. I use Claude and Cursor daily, and I've gotten pretty opinionated about the difference between "AI gave me an answer" and "AI gave me the _right_ answer", verifying the second one is basically my whole job.

Why ReadMe: I've spent my career on the support side of API-first products, and I've felt firsthand what the difference between good docs and bad docs does to a support queue. I'm applying because I want to be on the team that makes that difference for other people's developers.
