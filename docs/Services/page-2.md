Cats are enigmatic, self-possessed creatures that have lived alongside humans for roughly 10,000 years, first drawn to early agricultural settlements by the rodents that grain stores attracted. Unlike dogs, cats were never truly domesticated in the same directed way — they largely domesticated themselves, tolerating human proximity on their own terms. Today around 600 million domestic cats inhabit the world, yet retain strong instincts for hunting, territory, and independence. Their flexible spines, retractable claws, and acute senses make them exceptional predators. Beloved for their mysterious personalities, graceful movement, and occasional affection, cats have inspired art, mythology, and internet culture across every era of human civilization.
