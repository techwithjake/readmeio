Berries are nature's most vibrant and nutritious small fruits, encompassing strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, blackberries, and countless others. Packed with antioxidants, vitamins, and polyphenols, they rank among the most nutrient-dense foods available. Blueberries are celebrated for cognitive benefits, while strawberries deliver impressive doses of vitamin C. Many "berries" in common usage — like strawberries and raspberries — are botanically not true berries, while bananas and avocados technically are. Wild berries have fed humans for millennia, long before agriculture existed. Today they flavor jams, desserts, smoothies, and wines. Their intense colors signal phytonutrient richness, making them as beautiful as they are beneficial.
